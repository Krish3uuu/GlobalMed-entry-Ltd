1. The Post-Brexit Regulatory Status of European Medical Degrees

One of the most common questions prospective students and parents ask is whether Brexit has impacted the recognition of European medical degrees. The definitive answer is: European medical degrees continue to be fully recognized by the General Medical Council (GMC).

The UK's Medical Act 1983 and the GMC’s registration framework mandate that medical degrees awarded by accredited universities within the European Economic Area (EEA) meet the strict European directive requirements (Directive 2005/36/EC). These standards guarantee:

5,500 Hours Minimum

A minimum of 6 years and 5,500 hours of theoretical and practical university instruction.

Comprehensive Clinical Rotations

Mandatory bedside hospital clerkships across Internal Medicine, General Surgery, Pediatrics, and OB/GYN.

2. Understanding the UKMLA (Medical Licensing Assessment)

The UK Medical Licensing Assessment (UKMLA) was introduced by the GMC to establish a unified, objective benchmark for safe medical practice in the UK. Rather than subjecting overseas doctors to discriminatory barriers, the UKMLA is a universal assessment taken by both UK-trained doctors and international graduates alike.

The UKMLA consists of two distinct components:

Component 1: Applied Knowledge Test (AKT) 200 MCQs

A computer-based test consisting of 200 single-best-answer (SBA) questions testing clinical reasoning, diagnostic skills, and patient management aligned with UK National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) guidelines. Tests are sat at Pearson VUE test centers across the UK and internationally.

Component 2: Clinical & Professional Skills (CPSA) OSCE Format

A practical examination evaluating clinical history-taking, physical examination skills, acute patient management, and communication ethics. For European universities, universities incorporate GMC-approved CPSA formats into their final year clinical state licensing examinations.

4. Step-by-Step GMC Registration Roadmap

Following graduation, GlobalMed Entry Ltd’s dedicated Phase IV Alumni Team walks each graduate through the registration sequence:

1

EPIC Primary Medical Qualification Verification

Your official medical diploma and state exam certificates are verified through ECFMG's Electronic Portfolio of International Credentials (EPIC) directly with your European university rectorate.

2

GMC Online Account & Eligibility Submission

Creation of your GMC Online profile, submission of Certificate of Good Standing from the Bulgarian or relevant European Medical Association, and ID verification.

3

UKMLA AKT Booking & Examination

Booking your AKT exam slot at a local UK Pearson VUE test centre with comprehensive question bank access provided by GlobalMed Entry.

4

Full GMC Registration & Licence to Practise

Upon approval, your name is entered onto the official GMC Medical Register with full rights to write prescriptions, admit patients, and work in UK NHS trusts.

5

Commencing NHS Clinical Practice

Commencing your allocated Foundation Programme FY1 position or starting as an NHS Trust Grade House Officer.
