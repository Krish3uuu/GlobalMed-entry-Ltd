How To Apply To Study Medicine In Europe

A structured, doctor-led roadmap designed to eliminate paperwork errors, secure verified seats, and guarantee a smooth entry into top European medical faculties.

It can be extremely difficult applying to a university in a country you have never visited before especially with the paperwork and legal issues that you can stumble upon along the way. It is important that your application is managed with perfection and every obstacle is faced with experienced guidance to ensure a smooth sailing application process.

Applying to study medicine or dentistry in Europe has never been easier with the help and guidance that you can receive with GlobalMed Entry Ltd and the immense amount of resources that can help prepare you for the entrance exams or interviews if required. We will help you every step of the way and you can check out more details about services that will tell you exactly how we help our students throughout the whole process.

Transparent Step-by-Step Methodology

The Application Process

How our UK advisory team manages your dossier from initial contact through to university registration and clinical orientation abroad.

01 Initiation

Online Submission

We will email you our application forms and terms of service agreement/contract for you to fill out within a timely manner.

Stage 1 of 6 bolt 24h digital turnaround
02 Dossier Assembly

Submit The Forms

After completing the forms, send them back to us alongside a scanned copy of your passport and educational certificates for us to process until we receive the hard copies necessary for submission.

Stage 2 of 6 lock Encrypted portal
03 Quality Audit

Review Submission

Our specialised team will verify the forms are in order and let you know if any changes need to be made in order to continue the application. At this point, we will also provide further details such as entrance exam dates to choose.

Stage 3 of 6 verified 0% Error check
04 Legalisation

Gather Necessary Documents

Whilst you are preparing for the entrance exam using our online portal, gather all the relevant documents that our team advises you will need to submit to the university.

Stage 4 of 6 menu_book Portal access included
05 Examination

Entrance Exam/Interview

Travel to the country of your university choice to sit the entrance exam or to take an interview if required. Our team will be by your side to assist at all times.

Stage 5 of 6 badge On-site UK chaperones
06 Enrolment

Last Decision

You will be given a date when the university rankings are released to know if you have been successful and have received an admission. At this point, you should be ready to relocate abroad!

Stage 6 of 6 celebration Welcome to medical school
