Our Origin & Motivation

Why GlobalMed Entry Ltd Was Founded

Back in 2015, our co-founders Abdullah and Dr. Ade embarked on their ambition to study medicine abroad in Europe. Like thousands of British students every year, they turned to traditional student recruitment agencies for guidance.

What they discovered was deeply disheartening: traditional consultancies were run purely by administrative brokers with zero clinical background. Once the application fees were pocketed and admissions letters issued, students were abruptly abandoned in a foreign country to navigate language barriers, complex apartment leases, police registration, and grueling medical faculties completely on their own.

In 2017, while Abdullah was in his 3rd year of medical school in Bulgaria and Ade was advancing toward graduation, they decided to completely reinvent how European medical admissions should operate. They formed GlobalMed Entry Ltd with an unwavering student-first ethos: we guide you through admission, stand beside you through all 6 years of university, and mentor your transition into the UK National Health Service.

“We remember exactly how daunting it was landing in Sofia with two suitcases and no one to turn to. Our pledge is simple: no GlobalMed Entry student will ever face this journey alone.” — Dr. Ade & Abdullah, Co-Founders of GlobalMed Entry Ltd
