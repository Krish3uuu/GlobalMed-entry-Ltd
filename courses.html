Straightforward Pathways

Entry Requirements for Medical Studies in Bulgaria

Unlike UK medical schools with quota caps and 10:1 applicant ratios, Bulgarian faculties assess candidates objectively based on secondary science education and entrance testing.

military_tech 100% GlobalMed Entry Entrance Pass Guarantee

Our medical student applicants receive curated question banks, past university papers, online prep seminars, and mock tests with a proven 100% entrance pass rate.

01

Academic Qualifications

High school diploma, UK A-Levels, International Baccalaureate (IB), Scottish Highers, or Irish Leaving Certificate. Graduate applicants holding relevant science degrees are also welcome.

02

Biology & Chemistry

Applicants must have studied Biology and Chemistry in their secondary education. Specific minimum grade averages apply depending on EU or non-EU citizenship.

03

Science Entrance Exam

Multiple-choice examinations assessing core fundamental concepts in Biology and Chemistry. Tests are administered online with GlobalMed Entry guidance and prep modules.

04

Apostille & Translation

Academic and medical certificates must be apostilled by the UK Foreign Office and translated into Bulgarian. GlobalMed Entry executes this entire legal pipeline end-to-end.
