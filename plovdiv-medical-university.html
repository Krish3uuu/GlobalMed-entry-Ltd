University Profile

About Plovdiv Medical University

Established in 1945, Plovdiv Medical University has grown into one of Southeastern Europe’s benchmark academic institutions for health sciences. Located in Plovdiv—Bulgaria’s vibrant cultural hub and second-largest city—the university is composed of four principal faculties: the Faculty of Medicine, Faculty of Dental Medicine, Faculty of Pharmacy, and Faculty of Public Health, complemented by a dedicated Medical College and Department of Languages.

The university offers comprehensive, English-taught 6-year Medicine (MD) and 5-year Dental Medicine (DMD) programmes structured strictly according to the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS). Students complete 360 ECTS credits in Medicine, spanning preclinical foundation disciplines in Years 1–2, systemic pathological and clinical science in Years 3–5, and a comprehensive 52-week clinical state internship in Year 6.

Over 8,000 students from more than 50 nations study at the campus, creating a warm, collegiate, and truly international atmosphere. British and Irish students constitute the largest single demographic of foreign students in Plovdiv, benefiting from active student societies, peer mentorship, and extensive alumni networks already practicing in the UK National Health Service (NHS).

Admissions Guide

Plovdiv Medical University Application Process | Admission and Entry Requirements

Gaining admission to Plovdiv Medical University involves a transparent, merit-based four-stage procedure. GlobalMed Entry Ltd manages every administrative and legal milestone on your behalf.

1 Academic Requirements

Applicants must hold a completed High School Diploma (A-Levels, Scottish Highers, IB, or equivalent) with passing grades in Biology and Chemistry. The minimum combined average in sciences must exceed 62%.

2 Online Entrance Exam

Candidates sit an online computer-based exam consisting of multiple-choice questions (MCQs) in Biology and Chemistry, plus an English diagnostic test. Exams take place remotely between May and September.

3 Legalisation Pipeline

All academic diplomas, medical certificates, and police clearances must receive the UK FCDO Apostille, certified sworn translation into Bulgarian, and authentication at the Bulgarian Embassy. We handle this 100% in-house.

4 Ministry Visa & Enrolment

The university rectorate submits your verified file to the Bulgarian Ministry of Education to obtain your official Certificate of Acceptance, allowing you to secure your Type D Student Visa and finalize in-person matriculation.
